How to interpret these metrics
  • Cumulative return: historical price change over the selected period.
  • Volatility: higher values indicate more variable daily returns.
  • Maximum drawdown: the largest observed decline from a previous peak.
  • Sharpe ratio: historical excess return relative to volatility.

Historical estimates do not guarantee future performance.
